# Privacy Policy - World Clocks Chrome Extension

**Last Updated:** October 31, 2026

## Overview
**World Clocks** is a browser extension that displays customizable analog and digital clocks for global timezones in a toolbar popup. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection & Usage
- **No Personal Data Collected**: World Clocks does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII), browsing history, IP addresses, analytics, or telemetry.
- **No External Servers**: All timezone calculations are performed locally on your device using your browser's native ECMAScript `Intl` API. The extension makes zero network requests.

## Local Preferences Storage
- The extension uses Chrome's built-in `chrome.storage.sync` API solely to save your preferences:
  1. Your configured list of cities/timezones and their order
  2. Your selected mode (`dark` or `light`), colour theme, and clock font
  3. Your selected clock display modes (`analog` and/or `digital`)
- This data remains within your browser and Google Chrome sync profile and is never accessible to the developer or any third party.

## Third-Party Services
World Clocks does not integrate any third-party SDKs, trackers, advertisements, remote scripts, or remote fonts.
